SELLER'S SUBMISSION (evidence and argument):

1. The listing (order #4471): "AquaBlaster 3000 — neon orange water pistol, ages 6+, shoots water up to 8 metres, 900 ml tank." The listing text describes a toy water pistol in a bright neon orange color. It contains no reference to realistic styling, metal parts, or tactical accessories.
2. The product is molded entirely in neon orange plastic, including the tip, barrel, and grip. No part is black, metallic, or camouflage colored.
3. Under §2.3, brightly colored toy water pistols that cannot be mistaken for functional weapons are Allowed items and expressly NOT a Prohibited Category. The withheld ground cites §3 only; §3 applies to replicas a reasonable observer would mistake for functional weapons, which a neon orange water pistol is not.
4. No fraud flag under §5 exists on order #4471; the withholding notice cites no fraud ground.
Therefore the withholding had no basis in the governing terms and the payout must be released.
