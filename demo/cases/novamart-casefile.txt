CASE: NovaMart marketplace — payout withholding appeal No. 4471

GOVERNING TERMS (NovaMart Payout Policy, excerpts):
- §2.1 Payouts to sellers are released 7 days after delivery confirmation.
- §2.2 A payout may be withheld only if the listing falls in a Prohibited Category (§3) or the order is flagged for fraud (§5).
- §3 Prohibited Categories include: functional weapons; weapon replicas that a reasonable observer would mistake for functional weapons.
- §2.3 Toy exemption: brightly colored toy water pistols that cannot be mistaken for functional weapons are Allowed items and are NOT a Prohibited Category.
- §5 Fraud withholding requires a documented fraud flag on the order.

DECISION UNDER APPEAL: On 2026-11-02 the platform withheld the seller's payout of 1.0 NEAR for order #4471, stating the ground: "listing is a weapon replica (Prohibited Category §3)".

QUESTION PRESENTED: On the material in the submission, was the withholding justified under the governing terms? PASS means the submission prevails (the withholding was not justified and the payout must be released). FAIL means the withholding was justified.
