CASE: Meridian Grants — Milestone 2 judgment, grant G-117 (peristaltic pump prototype)

GOVERNING TERMS (Grant Agreement, Milestone 2):
The Milestone 2 payment of 0.8 NEAR is released only if the milestone report submitted by the grantee contains ALL of the following:
1. Endurance test results showing at least 200 completed pump cycles, with the measured flow rate.
2. A cost breakdown table of the prototype's parts and fabrication.
3. A reference (name or link) to the public data repository where the raw test logs are stored.
If any element is absent, the milestone is not met and the payment is withheld.

QUESTION PRESENTED: On the material in the submission, are the Milestone 2 terms satisfied? PASS means the milestone is met and the payment is released. FAIL means it is not met.
