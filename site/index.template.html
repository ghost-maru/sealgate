Sealgate — Escrow Verifier

Inspection-gated escrow on NEAR testnet. The chain holds hashes, a verdict, a TEE signature, and an encrypted response. Criteria and deliverable are never on-chain. This page reads the live contract and lets a party (or a judge with the demo record) verify a claim end-to-end, in the browser.

Verify a claim

The integrity stage is deterministic code: rebuild the canonical request from the record, compare hashes, verify the TEE signature, check the signer registry, and confirm the published verdict equals the signed verdict.

Decrypt the on-chain response (parties only)

Models: L0 Qwen/Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct · L1 z-ai/glm-5.3-flash (NEAR AI Cloud, Confidential TEE). Contract on NearBlocks (testnet). Source: the Sealgate repository README explains the security model — including what is not proven.
