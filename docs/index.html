Sealgate — Judge Platform Verifier

A general-purpose judge-AI platform on NEAR testnet: sealed-record judgments with TEE-signed verdicts, an appellate protocol, and escrow settlement for a 1% fee. The chain holds hashes, a verdict, a TEE signature, and an encrypted response. The record itself is never on-chain. This page reads the live contract and lets a party (or a judge with the demo record) verify a claim end-to-end, in the browser.
