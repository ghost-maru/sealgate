# Acceptance criteria: competitor report (full version)

The submission must be a market report covering all five competitors: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta and Epsilon.

For EACH competitor, its section must contain all three items:
1. the product name,
2. the price in JPY,
3. a maintenance note: one sentence about servicing or support.

The report must end with a summary price table with one row per competitor.
A section missing any of the three items fails the report.
