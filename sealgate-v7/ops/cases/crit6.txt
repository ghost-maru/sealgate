# Acceptance criteria: competitor report

The submission must be a market report covering all five competitors:
Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta and Epsilon.

Requirements:
1. One section per competitor describing its main product.
2. A price table with one row per competitor.
3. Coverage must be complete: a report that omits a competitor, or a
   price table that omits a row, fails.

Exception to requirement 3: if a competitor's price is not publicly
disclosed, its row may state "not disclosed" instead of a number,
provided the report names the source that was checked for the price.
A row that follows this exception counts as complete coverage.
